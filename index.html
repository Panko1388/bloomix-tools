Bloomix Keyword Research

An internal keyword research tool of Bloomix, an online retail company.

What it does

Bloomix Keyword Research connects to the Google Ads API on behalf of a Bloomix staff member who signs in with their own Google account. It reads keyword ideas, average monthly searches, year-over-year trends and top-of-page bid ranges from Google Ads Keyword Planner for the Google Ads accounts that Bloomix owns or manages.

We use these numbers to choose accurate product titles and descriptions for the products in our own online stores, so that shoppers searching on Google find the products that match what they are looking for.

What it does not do

The tool does not create, change or pause campaigns, ads, budgets or billing settings. It is not offered to the public, has no customer sign-up, and is used only by Bloomix staff.

Privacy
